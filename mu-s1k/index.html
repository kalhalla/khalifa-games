Tap play to start. Each part of your track is a layer. Tap + to add drums, an acid line, a bassline, chords, vocal chops or your own samples, and tap a layer to edit it. Every layer has a main and an alt pattern. In loop mode you hear every unmuted layer; on the song page you switch layers on and off, section by section, to build intros, drops and breaks. Drag or flick the data wheel to set the tempo; on an acid line it changes the selected note. Turn a knob by dragging up or down on it. On iPhone, flip the silent switch off if you hear nothing. Export a WAV from the song page. Your work saves on this device automatically.
