MU-S1K built-in vocal sounds

Cut, tuned and filtered from two recordings released under Creative Commons 0
(public domain) in the Sonic Pi sample set (github.com/sonic-pi-net/sonic-pi,
etc/samples):

- tbd_voctone: donated by The Black Dog under CC0.
  Used for: ooh, ah, oh, ee, hey (vox stab), yeah (vox chop), ohoh.
- ambi_choir: from freesound.org/people/Exsomniel/sounds/207809/, CC0.
  Used for: choir.

No attribution is required by CC0; it is given here as thanks.

Recorded drum kit and chord instruments

- kit/kick: Sonic Pi sample bd_haus (CC0, via freesound.org).
- kit/clap1, clap2, hat1, hat2, open, tamb: Versilian Community Sample Library
  (VCSL, github.com/sgossner/VCSL), CC0 - Claps, Hi-Hat Cymbal, Tambourine 1.
- piano/: VCSL Grand Piano, Steinway B (no sustain, close mics), CC0.
- fmpiano/: VCSL TX81Z FM Piano, CC0.
- vibes/: VCSL Vibraphone, soft mallets, CC0.
All trimmed, levelled and converted to MP3 for MU-S1K.
